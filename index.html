✨ Who Are We?

The Neuqua Valley Astronomy Club is a student-led community dedicated to exploring space, astrophysics, and stargazing. Whether you are an expert on black holes or just curious about what's out there in the universe, our club offers an engaging, welcoming space for everyone!

🚀 Why Join Astronomy Club?

Looking for a fun place to hang out after school? Here are just a few reasons to get involved:

🍬 Candy & Prizes

Win awesome prizes, enjoy free candy, and compete in fun space trivia and activities!

👥 Bring Friends

Hang out with your friends or make new ones who share your interest in science and space.

🤝 Naperville Non-Profits

Interact and connect with amazing non-profit organization partners all across Naperville!

🔭 Telescope Nights

Get hands-on experience using telescopes to view the Moon, planets, and deep-space objects.
